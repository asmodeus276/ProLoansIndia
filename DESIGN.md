## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premier lending and mortgage advisory platform tailored specifically for Indian borrowers seeking clarity, speed, and institutional reliability. Borrowing visually from top-tier banking institutions, the interface balances approachable vitality with uncompromised authority. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Trustworthy & Institutional:** Evokes the dependability of sovereign and tier-1 banking institutions while eliminating bureaucratic friction.
- **Dynamic & Action-Oriented:** Propels users forward through complex loan underwriting and qualification funnels without cognitive overload.
- **Transparent & Precise:** Delivers unambiguous loan metrics, interest calculations, EMI breakdowns, and document requirements.

### Visual Style
The design system operates under a **Modern Corporate Precision** ethos. It pairs crisp, daylight-white canvases with structural deep blue architecture and energetic orange catalysts. Surfaces rely on hairline borders, subtle warm-gray fills, and disciplined geometry. Violet, purple, lavender, and indigo undertones are strictly forbidden across every surface, state, and shadow to preserve an unadulterated blue-and-orange financial corporate palette.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a disciplined duo of energetic orange and deep corporate blue, grounded by clinical cool-slate neutrals and stark daylight white backgrounds. 

### Color Hierarchy
- **Primary Brand Orange (`#F26522` / `#EA580C`):** Reserved for primary calls-to-action, key conversion steps, active milestones, loan approval badges, and dynamic calculators.
- **Secondary Corporate Navy (`#0F2942` / `#1E3A8A`):** Anchors headers, navigation bars, structural typography, hero sections, and foundational financial statements.
- **Tertiary Royal Accent (`#2563EB`):** Applied to active states, contextual information banners, data links, and secondary interactive points where warm orange would create visual clutter.
- **Slate Neutrals (`#0F172A`, `#334155`, `#64748B`, `#94A3B8`, `#E2E8F0`, `#F8FAFC`):** Provide balanced grayscale tones. All grays are strictly slate/steel-biased with zero violet or purple undertones.

### Functional States
- **Success:** `#16A34A` (Clear emerald green for sanctioned amounts, verification ticks, KYC completion)
- **Warning:** `#F59E0B` (Amber for pending documents and EMI reminders)
- **Destructive:** `#DC2626` (Pure red for failed verifications, overdue payments, and application rejection)
- **Surface Pure:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Subtle:** `#F8FAFC`
- **Surface Card:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Border Default:** `#E2E8F0`
- **Border Focus:** `#0F2942`

## Typography

The typography pairings contrast the architectural clarity of **Manrope** for primary headers, quantitative loan stats, and value propositions with the systematic legibility of **Inter** for data grids, terms, and input forms.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Manrope Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (700, 800) bring stability to loan amounts, interest percentages, and section headers. High-value rupee figures (`₹`) utilize Manrope with tight kerning for immediate numerical impact.
- **Inter Body & Data:** Neutral, non-distracting rendering across all resolutions. Form inputs, amortization tables, and legal disclosures maintain high reading comfort through generous line heights (1.5x minimum).
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered in medium and semi-bold weights for rapid form completion, error handling, and stepper flows.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured, predictable 12-column layout creates institutional order. Spacing uses an 8px grid baseline with half-step increments (4px) reserved exclusively for compact financial tables and input fields.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width container of 1280px, 24px gutters, and 32px canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid, 20px gutters, and 24px margins.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, 16px gutters, and 16px horizontal margins.

### Vertical Rhythm & Density
Financial forms demand moderate density. Group fields with 16px gaps within fieldsets, separating fieldsets by 32px. Dashboards containing EMI sliders and rate comparisons use clean 24px component padding to prevent accidental touch interactions on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system relies on clean planar layering, crisp outlines, and controlled, neutral slate drop shadows. Coloured shadows are forbidden, ensuring zero purple or indigo optical contamination.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** `#F8FAFC` base background with `#FFFFFF` panels separated by crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders. Used for static content sections, amortization data tables, and background cards.
- **Level 1 (Interactive Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05)` and a subtle `#E2E8F0` border. Used for loan feature cards, partner bank cards, and inactive form containers.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Containers):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06)` with a stroke change to `#2563EB` or `#F26522`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Calculators):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08)`. Backdrops use `rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.6)` blur (4px) to retain connection to the primary corporate blue.

## Shapes

The system uses a **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** profile. Standard corners are set to `0.5rem` (8px), container cards to `1rem` (16px), and prominent promotional callouts to `1.5rem` (24px).

This balance maintains the crisp, structured geometry of a financial institution while softening actionable elements like loan calculators, eligibility gauges, and inquiry buttons for a friendly, modern experience. Pill styling is strictly reserved for status badges, tags, and chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Apply / Calculate / Continue):** Solid `#F26522` background, white typography (`label-md`), 8px border radius, 12px 24px padding. Hover: `#EA580C`. Active: `#C2410C`. Focus: 3px outer ring in `#F26522` at 30% opacity.
- **Secondary Action (View Breakdown / Bank Details):** Solid `#0F2942` background, white typography, hover: `#1E3A8A`.
- **Outline Action:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #0F2942`, `#0F2942` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height: 48px for standard mobile-friendly touch targets.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Radius: 8px.
- Focus: `1.5px solid #0F2942` with zero purple hue; optional secondary blue glow `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- Rupee prefix (`₹`) and percentage suffix (`%`) locked to slate-500 (`#64748B`) inside input prefix containers.

### Cards
- **Loan Product Cards:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` container, 16px radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, 24px internal padding. Top accent bar in `#F26522` or `#0F2942` indicates loan type (Home, Personal, Business).
- **Metric Highlight Cards:** Elevated display showcasing Approved Amount, Tenor, and Interest with Manrope display numbers and Inter slate captions.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills (Pre-Approved, Instant KYC):** Pill radius (9999px), 4px 12px padding, `label-sm`.
  - Approved/Low Rate: `#DCFCE7` background, `#15803D` text.
  - Active Selection: `#0F2942` background, `#FFFFFF` text.
  - Featured: `#FFEDD5` background, `#C2410C` text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 20px x 20px. Radius: 4px for checkboxes, 50% circular for radio buttons.
- Border: `1.5px solid #94A3B8`.
- Checked: Fill `#0F2942` with white checkmark/dot. Active state can optionally use `#F26522` for agreement/consent checkboxes.

### Loan Sliders & Range Controls
- Track: 6px height, `#E2E8F0` background.
- Fill: `#F26522` progress indicator.
- Thumb: 24px circular `#FFFFFF` grip with `2px solid #F26522` and Level 1 elevation for effortless touch dragging on mobile devices.

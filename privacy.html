Privacy Policy & Borrower Data Protection Charter

Effective Date: 1st January 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

At Proloan India (Proloan Financial Services & Consultancy) (referred to as "we", "our", "us"), respecting and guarding the privacy of your personal and financial information is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and sharing of personal data obtained through our website, mobile interfaces, doorstep consultations, and customer care touchpoints.

1. Information We Collect

To evaluate your eligibility across 50+ Scheduled Commercial Banks and RBI-registered NBFCs, we collect:

  • Identity & Demographic Data: Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Father's/Spouse's Name, PAN, and Aadhaar (masked).
  • Contact Coordinates: Current and Permanent Residential Address, Mobile Number, and Email Address.
  • Financial & Employment Details: Salary slips, Form 16, ITR acknowledgments, business P&L accounts, bank account statements, employer name, business vintage, and registered GSTIN.
  • Credit History Information: Credit scores and bureau reports retrieved with your explicit consent from authorized credit information companies (CIBIL, Experian, CRIF High Mark, Equifax).
  • Property Documents: Title deeds, chain agreements, and sanction maps solely for valuation in Home Loans and Loan Against Property (LAP).

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate loan facilitation activities:

  • Assessing loan eligibility and matching your profile with partner banks offering lowest interest rates.
  • Facilitating doorstep document collection, preliminary underwriting verification, and multi-bank file logins.
  • Communicating milestone progress, sanction letters, and disbursal updates via SMS, Phone, and WhatsApp.
  • Preventing fraud, identity theft, and ensuring compliance with Know Your Customer (KYC) guidelines.

3. Zero Data Sale Guarantee & Disclosure to Third Parties

We unequivocally guarantee that Proloan India NEVER sells, rents, leases, or trades your personal information to third-party telemarketers or external advertising networks. Your documents and details are shared exclusively with:

  • Designated underwriting and credit operations teams of RBI-regulated partner banks and NBFCs where your loan file is submitted.
  • Licensed Credit Information Companies (CIBIL/Experian) upon your authorization for score retrieval.
  • Statutory, regulatory, or law enforcement authorities only when mandated under applicable Indian law.

4. Data Security & Storage Protocols

We implement industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption for data transmission. Hardcopy photocopies collected during doorstep pickup are secured under dual-custody controls and dispatched directly to the evaluating lender. We never retain original property or identity documents.

5. Your Data Rights & Consent Withdrawal

As a data principal under the DPDP Act, you possess the right to access, review, correct, or request the purging of your stored personal data after conclusion of your loan process. You may revoke consent by emailing our Data Protection Officer at privacy@proloanindia.com.
